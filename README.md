## 👋 What I fix

I'm a senior mobile engineer (Flutter, React Native, Swift, Kotlin) who solves difficult production problems: apps that break offline, lose data on sync, fail on real-world edge cases, mishandle payments and subscriptions — and now, AI features that have to respect privacy. I own the work from architecture to App Store and Google Play release.

<br>

## 🧩 Problems I've solved

| The problem | What I did | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Wrong prayer times in Finland & Norway.** Twilight never fully ends in a Nordic summer, so standard formulas give wrong or missing times. | Wrote an offline prayer-time engine in Dart with high-latitude rules (built at Devxhub) | [Muslim Times Pro](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/muslim-times-pro-prayer-quran/id6740039144) gives correct times year-round, fully offline |
| **Receipts generic scanners couldn't read** (Tanto, Brazil). Every failed scan meant manual entry and slow refunds. | Custom QR/barcode scanner → SEFAZ tax-agency validation → policy checks → instant Pix payout | Photo of a receipt → verified invoice → employee paid |
| **Learning apps that broke without internet** for students in 5 countries. | Led 6 developers; built the Firebase/SQLite offline sync layer with deterministic conflict resolution | 100,000+ students · **4.7★ at 100K+ daily users** |
| **Subscriptions charging users twice** (iOS). | Fixed StoreKit 2 pending-state and duplicate-purchase race conditions | One purchase = one entitlement; App Store approved |
| **Lawyers losing billable hours** and unable to use cloud trackers. | Built [Hourwise](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hourwise-billable-hours/id6811420308) solo: passive capture, on-device classifier, local-first storage, LEDES exports | Live on the Mac App Store; data never leaves the Mac |
| **One client, five apps' worth of features** (Farenow, US). | Designed one modular app: rides, delivery, bookings, consultations, marketplace | One codebase, both stores |
